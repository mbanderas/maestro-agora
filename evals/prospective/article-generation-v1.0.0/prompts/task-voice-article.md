/agora inform Write a 220 to 300 word short article for team leads about what an intake pilot can and cannot show. Return only the article.

Style: calm, skeptical, direct. Use short paragraphs, plain verbs, and no rhetorical questions. The two samples below show style only. Do not transfer their facts, examples, people, or opinions into the article.

Voice sample 1: "The workshop ended early. That gave us time to check the drawings. It did not tell us whether the new layout would work on a busy day."

Voice sample 2: "I would start with the records we can inspect. The sales team's confidence is useful context, but it is not a count of completed orders."

Article facts:
- Our team tested a new intake form for two weeks.
- Five team leads used it.
- They reported that duplicate requests were easier to spot.
- We did not count duplicate requests before and after the pilot.
- Our recommendation is to use the pilot to decide what to measure next, not to claim a reduction in duplicate requests.

Keep the report attributed to the team leads. Do not claim a measured improvement or create a personal story, customer, quote, statistic, or opinion beyond these facts.
