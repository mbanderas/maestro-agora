/agora --no-voice

Write the signature panel for River Step Coalition's petition asking the Vila Nova council to add a signal-controlled pedestrian crossing at the junction of Avenida das Flores and Rua do Mercado. The petition makes only that request and will be submitted to the council on 20 October 2026. A signer supplies full name, municipality, and postcode. Selecting the signature control adds the signature immediately; there is no email-confirmation step. The public petition displays the signer's first name and municipality, but not surname or postcode. A required checkbox confirms the signer understands that display. A separate unchecked checkbox permits campaign-update emails. The signature cannot be edited in place; a removal request sent by email before 18 October will be processed before submission. Removal requests must be sent to remove@riverstep.pt. The confirmation state shows that the signature was added and provides that removal email address. Output a petition heading, 50-70 word case, data-use summary, required-display consent line, optional-update consent line, signature control, and confirmation state. Do not invent collision data, council support, signature totals, legal effect, urgency, or broader campaign goals.
