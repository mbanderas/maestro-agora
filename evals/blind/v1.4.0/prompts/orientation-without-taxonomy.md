# Request

Write the hero for this company's public homepage: one headline, one supporting line, and one call to action. The page is the first thing a visitor sees.

## Supplied facts

- Ashcombe Reclamation strips slate from roofs being replaced, sorts the salvaged slates by size and thickness, and sells them to builders and conservation contractors.
- Salvaged slates are graded into three sizes. Within a size, thickness varies, so the company sorts each batch by thickness as well, because a roofer laying a repair needs slates that match what is already on the roof.
- The company holds stock from named quarries that closed decades ago and are no longer worked. When a batch from one of those quarries is sold, no more is available.
- It will match a sample: a customer sends one slate from their roof and the company finds the closest stock it holds, or tells the customer it holds nothing close.
- Minimum order is one pallet. It does not sell single slates and does not deliver outside the mainland.
- Internally the company describes itself as a heritage material recovery and reintroduction operation. That phrase appears in its grant applications and on its trade-body listing.
- Most first-time visitors arrive from a search for slate matching or roof slate repair and have not heard of the company.
- The call to action leads to a form where a visitor enters a slate size and a photograph and asks whether matching stock is held. A reply comes within two working days.
- No sales, volume, customer-count, or price figure is available.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent prices, volumes, customer numbers, or environmental claims.
- The grant applications and the trade-body listing are not being rewritten here.
