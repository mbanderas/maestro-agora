# Request

Write a closing block for a public marketing page: one short heading, one sentence, and the two calls to action described below.

## Supplied facts

- The company sells a scheduling service to independent dental practices.
- The first destination is a two-minute questionnaire that returns a written summary of how many appointment slots the practice left unfilled last month. It asks for the practice postcode and opening hours. It does not ask for payment details and does not create an account.
- The second destination is a thirty-minute video call with a member of the sales team, booked through a calendar.
- Both are free.
- No result, saving, or benchmark figure is available.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent urgency, scarcity, results, or social proof.
- Produce exactly two calls to action, one for each destination.
- A reader must be able to tell the two destinations apart from the labels alone.
