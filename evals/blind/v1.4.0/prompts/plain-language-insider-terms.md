# Request

Write the opening section for a public product page. Use about 90 words. The readers are facilities managers at mid-sized manufacturing sites. They are not employees of this company and have not read its documentation.

## Supplied facts

- The product monitors compressed-air lines and reports where pressurised air is escaping.
- It compares the volume a compressor produces with the volume the connected tools draw, every five minutes.
- When the gap between the two stays above the site's configured limit for three consecutive readings, it opens a work item and assigns it to the maintenance lead on shift.
- The company refers to this internally as "delta-persistence gating", and to the resulting work item as a "variance record".
- The comparison only covers lines where the customer has fitted a flow meter. Lines without a meter are not monitored.
- No figure for recovered energy, cost, or leak-repair time is available.
- A working link exists to a page that lists the supported meter models.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent savings, percentages, timings, customer counts, or comparisons to other products.
- The material limit about unmetered lines must survive.
- End with one call to action.
