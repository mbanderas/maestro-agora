Write a case study about a project that did not meet its primary target.

Facts: the team aimed to increase completion of a six-step onboarding flow from 38 to 50 percent. After launch, completion reached 41 percent, with a confidence interval overlapping the baseline. Support contacts about identity verification fell from 120 to 54 per month. Interviews showed that the new explanation resolved one confusion, but pricing remained the largest abandonment reason. The team retained the explanation and reverted two other changes.

Audience: product leaders evaluating the team's judgment. Do not recast the primary target as achieved. Show result, uncertainty, useful secondary evidence, decision, and next step.
