Our team changed one part of its process after staff noticed that customers sometimes arrived without the order number needed at the counter. Staff now ask for the order number in the return instructions before a customer visits. The team also started recording the reason for each return so managers can see which product questions come up repeatedly. We have not measured whether either change reduced repeat contacts.

Write in ordinary, direct language for a busy manager. Explain what the team noticed, what it changed, and what another manager could consider. Do not add a customer story, quote, number, result, or process step we did not supply.
