First-party account from our team:
- In March, we migrated our support inbox to a new ticket system.
- Two support reps spent 11 hours reconciling 76 duplicate tickets after the import.
- We paused the next import until we had mapped the old ticket identifiers to the new system.
- Our operations lead's lesson: a migration plan needs a data check before a launch date is promised.
- We did not measure customer satisfaction, resolution time, or revenue impact.

Use the actual event and decision to explain the lesson. Keep the operations lead's opinion attributed. Do not add customers, quotations, effects, figures, or steps we did not provide. Do not turn the 11 hours into a saving or claim the data check eliminated duplicates.
