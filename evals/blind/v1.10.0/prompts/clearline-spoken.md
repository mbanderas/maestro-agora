Audience: clinic reception teams.

Supplied facts:
- ClearLine shows unanswered calls in one queue.
- Staff can assign a call to a teammate.
- The teammate can mark it returned.
- ClearLine does not record calls or contact patients automatically.
- The video ends by showing the queue. No CTA is requested.

Start directly with the supplied product facts. Do not invent a busy shift, missed-call problem, risk of losing track, or decision about who should handle a call. Do not say the teammate returned the call. The ending may say only that the queue shows the unanswered calls together; do not add `all` or say the view returns to the queue. Do not say the queue displays return status or keeps marked calls. Return only the script. Make it easy to say aloud. Do not add an abstract line about calls remaining in the team's hands or the team keeping control. Do not add search-style headings, a summary, an imperative ending, patient outcomes, or invented workflow steps.
