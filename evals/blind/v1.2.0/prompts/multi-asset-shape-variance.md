# Request

Write four section headings and their opening sentences for one public overview page, plus the page's single call to action. Each section covers one of the four capabilities below.

## Supplied facts

- The company sells a fleet maintenance service to regional haulage operators.
- Capability one: it reads odometer and fault-code data from each vehicle's onboard port and records when a service interval is passed.
- Capability two: it compares the parts a garage quoted against the parts the manufacturer specifies for that vehicle and model year, and flags any that differ.
- Capability three: it holds each vehicle's inspection certificates and warns the transport manager fourteen days before one expires.
- Capability four: it groups vehicles due for service in the same week so a manager can book them into one garage visit.
- The call to action leads to a page where a manager enters their fleet size and vehicle makes and receives a written estimate of monthly cost. It requires no payment details.
- No result, saving, downtime, or customer-count figure is available.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent savings, time reductions, percentages, or customer numbers.
- The four headings appear on the same page and will be read together.
- One call to action for the whole page.
