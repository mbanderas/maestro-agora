# Request

Write the opening section of a landing page for this product, plus the one action it asks for. Around one hundred words.

## Supplied facts

- Calder Water Systems sells filtration equipment to small food-manufacturing plants.
- Mineral scale builds up inside a plant's heat exchangers over months. As it thickens, the exchanger transfers less heat and the plant runs its boiler longer to hit the same temperature.
- Calder's equipment measures the water hardness entering the exchanger every hour and adjusts the dosing rate of a scale inhibitor to match it, instead of dosing at a fixed rate.
- Calder's internal name for this adjustment loop is Hardness-Matched Dosing. The name appears in its engineering documentation and on the equipment label.
- The equipment fits plants with a single boiler loop. It does not fit plants running more than one loop from a shared feed.
- Installation takes one working day and requires the loop to be shut down for four hours.
- The action available is a site survey: an engineer visits, measures the incoming water and the current dosing setup, and returns a written report on whether the equipment fits the plant. The survey is free and creates no obligation.
- Buyers are plant managers at bakeries, dairies, and drinks bottlers.
- Research on this audience found that most plant managers know their boiler runs longer in some months than others, and have not previously connected that to scale in the exchanger. They do not use the words scale inhibitor or dosing rate unprompted.
- No energy saving, cost, downtime, or customer-count figure is available.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent savings, percentages, payback periods, or customer numbers.
- The equipment label and the engineering documentation are not being rewritten here.
