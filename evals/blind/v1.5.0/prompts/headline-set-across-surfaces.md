# Request

Write the page headline and its subheading for the course sales page, the browser and search-result title for the same page, the subject line and preview line for the announcement email, and the text of one social post that links to the page. All five surfaces describe the same course.

## Supplied facts

- Ridgeway Apiary School sells an online beekeeping course for people who already keep one or two hives and want to keep more.
- The course runs for eight weeks, one recorded lesson per week, each lesson between twenty and thirty minutes.
- Every lesson is followed by a live question session that is recorded for anyone who cannot attend.
- The course covers splitting a colony, queen rearing, disease inspection, winter feeding, and moving hives to a second site.
- It does not cover honey processing, bottling, labelling, or selling.
- The next intake opens on the first Monday of March and the course price is fixed for that intake.
- Learners keep access to the recordings for two years after the course ends.
- No pass rate, learner count, satisfaction figure, or honey-yield result is available.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Do not invent yields, learner numbers, testimonials, or results.
- The five surfaces will be published together and may be seen by the same person.
- The email preview line is displayed after the subject line and is not guaranteed to appear at all.
