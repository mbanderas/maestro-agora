Write a 90-second public science video script about seasons.

Audience: adults without specialist astronomy knowledge. A supplied pre-session survey of this exact audience found that 61 of 100 respondents believed summer happens because Earth is closer to the Sun.

Facts: seasons are driven primarily by Earth's axial tilt, which changes sun angle and day length across hemispheres. Earth is slightly closer to the Sun during Northern Hemisphere winter. The script may use a supported misconception, but it must correct it clearly and explain the replacement model.

Return narration only. Do not claim the belief is universal or that the hook improves retention.
