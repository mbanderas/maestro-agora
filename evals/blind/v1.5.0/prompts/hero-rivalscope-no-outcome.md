Write the homepage hero for RivalScope, a competitive research product.

Audience: B2B product marketing leaders comparing competitive-intelligence tools. Traffic is cold and direct. Desired action: open a sample competitor report.

Facts: RivalScope imports CRM win/loss records with named competitors and decision reasons. It monitors public competitor pricing, product pages, and release notes. It groups buyer feedback by competitor and decision criterion. It produces comparison reports, battlecard inputs, and recommended messaging changes. The sample report explains where competitors won and recommends ways to respond.

Limits: there is no measured evidence of improved win rate, revenue, or market share. RivalScope cannot guarantee that a customer wins a future deal.

Return one eyebrow if useful, headline, subhead, primary CTA, and one line of optional proof or qualification microcopy.
