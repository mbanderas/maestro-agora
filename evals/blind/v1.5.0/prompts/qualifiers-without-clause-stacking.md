# Request

Write about 70 words of public body copy explaining what the service reports and what it cannot report. The readers are compliance officers at insurance brokerages.

## Supplied facts

- The service checks whether each adviser at a brokerage held a valid licence on the date they gave advice.
- It reads licence status from the national register and from the brokerage's own staff records, and reports any date where the two disagree.
- Register data is refreshed once every twenty-four hours, so a licence change made today may not appear until tomorrow.
- The check covers only advisers the brokerage has added to its staff list.
- Results cover advice dated from 1 January 2023 onward. Earlier dates are not held.
- A disagreement between the two sources is a prompt to investigate. It is not a finding of misconduct and the service does not determine whether a rule was broken.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts above.
- Every limit above is material and must survive in the copy.
- Do not invent regulatory consequences, penalties, or outcomes.
- Do not write a disclaimer paragraph in legal register.
