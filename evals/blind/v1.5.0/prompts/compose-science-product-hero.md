Write a homepage hero for SpectraCheck, software that flags possible contamination peaks in laboratory spectra.

Audience: analytical chemistry lab managers. Desired action: review the validation dataset.

Facts: the model highlights peaks that differ from a laboratory's accepted reference spectra and shows the reference and observed signal side by side. Validation used 12,000 labeled spectra from three instrument models. Sensitivity was 91 percent and specificity 94 percent in that dataset. Performance on other instruments and sample types is unknown. A scientist must review every flag. The product does not identify the contaminant or certify sample safety.

Return a commercially strong hero that preserves the material scientific limits without reading like an abstract.
