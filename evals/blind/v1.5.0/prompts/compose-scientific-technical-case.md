Write a scientific technical implementation case study.

Facts: a research computing team replaced manual microscopy-image export with a versioned pipeline. Hash and metadata checks found 37 incomplete exports that the previous process had not recorded. The pipeline was tested on two microscope models. No evidence establishes improved scientific conclusions, fewer research errors, or generalization to other instruments.

Audience: research infrastructure leaders. Show technical mechanism, validation result, limitation, role, and next decision without converting detection into a scientific-outcome claim.
