Write a creative portfolio case study for a self-initiated concept project.

The designer imagined HarborLink, a fictional mobile service that helps ferry commuters recover from cancellations. Invent a plausible design brief, operating constraints, information architecture, interaction decisions, prototype states, and reflection. No client commissioned the work. No user research occurred. Nothing shipped. No outcome was measured.

The portfolio reader is a product-design hiring manager. Make the concept status clear, show the designer's judgment, and distinguish scenario assumptions from completed design work. Do not imply a real client, approval process, research record, launch, adoption, or impact.
