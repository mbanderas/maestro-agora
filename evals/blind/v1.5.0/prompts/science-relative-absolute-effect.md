Write a patient-facing explanation of a trial result without giving personal medical advice.

In a randomized trial, an event occurred in 2 of every 1,000 participants receiving the intervention and 1 of every 1,000 receiving the comparator during one year. A press release describes this as a 100 percent increase. The confidence interval is wide and includes effects smaller and larger than the point estimate. Serious adverse events did not differ clearly between groups.

Explain absolute and relative change, uncertainty, practical significance, and why a clinician still needs the full evidence and patient context.
