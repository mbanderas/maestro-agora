Write a 170 to 220 word section titled "Strength of evidence" for a clinical-guideline working group. One randomized controlled trial enrolled 186 adults for 12 weeks. The event rate was 20.0% in the control group and 16.4% in the intervention group, relative risk 0.82, 95% confidence interval 0.63 to 1.07. Attrition was 11% in the control group and 18% in the intervention group. The trial was not preregistered and has not been independently replicated. One observational cohort reported a change in the same direction, but treatment selection and baseline severity were not controlled. Assess how strong the evidence is, separate what the studies show from what remains uncertain, and state what further result would materially change the conclusion. Do not give treatment advice.
