/agora --no-voice

Write the final cancellation screen for Looplamp, a subscription library of motion-design assets. The customer is on a EUR 12 monthly plan and has already passed any refund period. The next renewal is 3 September 2026. Confirming cancellation stops that renewal and leaves downloads and project licenses available until 2 September 2026 at 23:59 Europe/Berlin. Previously downloaded assets remain licensed for projects created before access ends. Cancellation does not refund the current billing period and does not delete the account or saved collections. Reactivation before access ends resumes the same monthly price and renewal date; reactivation later uses the price offered at that time. The screen has one control that confirms cancellation and one that returns to the library. Confirmation takes effect immediately and opens a cancellation-receipt state; no support call or survey is required. Output the current-plan line, consequence summary, three dated or legal bullets, two control labels, and the receipt-state message. Do not add discounts, pauses, guilt, usage claims, alternative plans, or a mandatory reason field.
